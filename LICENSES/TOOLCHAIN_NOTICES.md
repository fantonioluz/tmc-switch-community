# Toolchain e fontes dos avisos

A lista de pacotes usados está em `toolchain/installed-packages.txt`.
Os avisos de libpng, mbedTLS e zlib foram copiados dos pacotes locais devkitPro.
Os textos GPL/LGPL vieram de `/usr/share/common-licenses/` da distribuição de build.

- `nlohmann-json-MIT.txt`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nlohmann/json/v3.11.3/LICENSE.MIT
- `toolchain/SDL2-LICENSE.txt`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/libsdl-org/SDL/release-2.28.5/LICENSE.txt
- `toolchain/curl-COPYING.txt`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/curl/curl/curl-7_69_1/COPYING
- `toolchain/libnx-LICENSE.txt`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/switchbrew/libnx/v4.12.0/LICENSE.md
- `toolchain/GCC-RUNTIME-EXCEPTION.txt`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gcc-mirror/gcc/master/COPYING.RUNTIME
- `toolchain/Mesa-license.html`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chaotic-cx/mesa-mirror/mesa-20.1.0/docs/license.html
